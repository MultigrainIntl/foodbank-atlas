Kansas Food Bank — Food Need & Funding Brief

Wichita & central, western and southeast Kansas · generated September 8, 2026

Across Wichita & central, western and southeast Kansas, the Food-Need Atlas scores every one of 446 neighborhoods (census tracts of ~4,000 people) on poverty, share under 200% of the federal poverty line, SNAP receipt, and low income. 26 neighborhoods — about 72,784 people — register high food need and are where distribution, mobile pantries, and the right product mix return the most.

SNAP gap: an estimated 128,359 residents across Wichita & central, western and southeast Kansas appear income-eligible for SNAP (under ~130% of poverty) but are likely not enrolled — unclaimed federal grocery dollars that would directly ease demand on the food bank, and a concrete outreach target and funding argument.
