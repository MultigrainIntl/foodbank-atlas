Food Bank of the Rockies — Food Need & Funding Brief

Denver, Colorado & Wyoming · generated September 8, 2026

Across Denver, Colorado & Wyoming, the Food-Need Atlas scores every one of 993 neighborhoods (census tracts of ~4,000 people) on poverty, share under 200% of the federal poverty line, SNAP receipt, and low income. 80 neighborhoods — about 327,946 people — register high food need and are where distribution, mobile pantries, and the right product mix return the most.

SNAP gap: an estimated 198,107 residents across Denver, Colorado & Wyoming appear income-eligible for SNAP (under ~130% of poverty) but are likely not enrolled — unclaimed federal grocery dollars that would directly ease demand on the food bank, and a concrete outreach target and funding argument.
