Oregon Food Bank — Food Need & Funding Brief

Oregon (statewide, 36 counties) + Clark County, WA · generated September 4, 2026

Across Oregon (statewide, 36 counties) + Clark County, WA, the Food-Need Atlas scores every one of 1,107 neighborhoods (census tracts of ~4,000 people) on poverty, share under 200% of the federal poverty line, SNAP receipt, and low income. 42 neighborhoods — about 165,539 people — register high food need and are where distribution, mobile pantries, and the right product mix return the most.

SNAP gap: an estimated 101,182 residents across Oregon (statewide, 36 counties) + Clark County, WA appear income-eligible for SNAP (under ~130% of poverty) but are likely not enrolled — unclaimed federal grocery dollars that would directly ease demand on the food bank, and a concrete outreach target and funding argument.
