Harry Chapin Food Bank of Southwest Florida — Food Need & Funding Brief

Southwest Florida (Charlotte, Collier, Glades, Hendry, Lee) · generated September 1, 2026

Across Southwest Florida (Charlotte, Collier, Glades, Hendry, Lee), the Food-Need Atlas scores every one of 388 neighborhoods (census tracts of ~4,000 people) on poverty, share under 200% of the federal poverty line, SNAP receipt, and low income. 32 neighborhoods — about 120,068 people — register high food need and are where distribution, mobile pantries, and the right product mix return the most.

SNAP gap: an estimated 99,440 residents across Southwest Florida (Charlotte, Collier, Glades, Hendry, Lee) appear income-eligible for SNAP (under ~130% of poverty) but are likely not enrolled — unclaimed federal grocery dollars that would directly ease demand on the food bank, and a concrete outreach target and funding argument.
