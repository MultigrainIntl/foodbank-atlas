Philabundance — Food Need & Funding Brief

Philadelphia, Pennsylvania & South Jersey · generated September 8, 2026

Across Philadelphia, Pennsylvania & South Jersey, the Food-Need Atlas scores every one of 1,364 neighborhoods (census tracts of ~4,000 people) on poverty, share under 200% of the federal poverty line, SNAP receipt, and low income. 130 neighborhoods — about 533,639 people — register high food need and are where distribution, mobile pantries, and the right product mix return the most.

SNAP gap: an estimated 165,734 residents across Philadelphia, Pennsylvania & South Jersey appear income-eligible for SNAP (under ~130% of poverty) but are likely not enrolled — unclaimed federal grocery dollars that would directly ease demand on the food bank, and a concrete outreach target and funding argument.
