Food Bank For New York City — Food Need & Funding Brief

New York City (five boroughs) · generated September 2, 2026

Across New York City (five boroughs), the Food-Need Atlas scores every one of 2,239 neighborhoods (census tracts of ~4,000 people) on poverty, share under 200% of the federal poverty line, SNAP receipt, and low income. 271 neighborhoods — about 1,219,803 people — register high food need and are where distribution, mobile pantries, and the right product mix return the most.

SNAP gap: an estimated 302,346 residents across New York City (five boroughs) appear income-eligible for SNAP (under ~130% of poverty) but are likely not enrolled — unclaimed federal grocery dollars that would directly ease demand on the food bank, and a concrete outreach target and funding argument.
