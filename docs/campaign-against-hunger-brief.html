The Campaign Against Hunger — Food Need & Funding Brief

Brooklyn / Kings County, New York · generated September 1, 2026

Across Brooklyn / Kings County, New York, the Food-Need Atlas scores every one of 778 neighborhoods (census tracts of ~4,000 people) on poverty, share under 200% of the federal poverty line, SNAP receipt, and low income. 79 neighborhoods — about 337,916 people — register high food need and are where distribution, mobile pantries, and the right product mix return the most.

SNAP gap: an estimated 96,877 residents across Brooklyn / Kings County, New York appear income-eligible for SNAP (under ~130% of poverty) but are likely not enrolled — unclaimed federal grocery dollars that would directly ease demand on the food bank, and a concrete outreach target and funding argument.
