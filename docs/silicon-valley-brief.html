Second Harvest of Silicon Valley — Food Need & Funding Brief

Santa Clara & San Mateo counties, California · generated September 2, 2026

Across Santa Clara & San Mateo counties, California, the Food-Need Atlas scores every one of 580 neighborhoods (census tracts of ~4,000 people) on poverty, share under 200% of the federal poverty line, SNAP receipt, and low income. 22 neighborhoods — about 83,671 people — register high food need and are where distribution, mobile pantries, and the right product mix return the most.

SNAP gap: an estimated 84,918 residents across Santa Clara & San Mateo counties, California appear income-eligible for SNAP (under ~130% of poverty) but are likely not enrolled — unclaimed federal grocery dollars that would directly ease demand on the food bank, and a concrete outreach target and funding argument.
