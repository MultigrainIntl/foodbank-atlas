Feeding Northeast Florida — Food Need & Funding Brief

Jacksonville & Northeast Florida · generated September 10, 2026

Across Jacksonville & Northeast Florida, the Food-Need Atlas scores every one of 463 neighborhoods (census tracts of ~4,000 people) on poverty, share under 200% of the federal poverty line, SNAP receipt, and low income. 34 neighborhoods — about 140,187 people — register high food need and are where distribution, mobile pantries, and the right product mix return the most.

SNAP gap: an estimated 139,572 residents across Jacksonville & Northeast Florida appear income-eligible for SNAP (under ~130% of poverty) but are likely not enrolled — unclaimed federal grocery dollars that would directly ease demand on the food bank, and a concrete outreach target and funding argument.
