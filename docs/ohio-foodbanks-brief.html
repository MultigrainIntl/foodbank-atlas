Ohio Association of Foodbanks — Food Need & Funding Brief

Ohio (statewide, 88 counties) · generated September 3, 2026

Across Ohio (statewide, 88 counties), the Food-Need Atlas scores every one of 3,152 neighborhoods (census tracts of ~4,000 people) on poverty, share under 200% of the federal poverty line, SNAP receipt, and low income. 221 neighborhoods — about 540,276 people — register high food need and are where distribution, mobile pantries, and the right product mix return the most.

SNAP gap: an estimated 613,181 residents across Ohio (statewide, 88 counties) appear income-eligible for SNAP (under ~130% of poverty) but are likely not enrolled — unclaimed federal grocery dollars that would directly ease demand on the food bank, and a concrete outreach target and funding argument.
