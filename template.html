How to use it

Neighborhood, not county. This splits the county's food-insecurity figure into __NTRACTS__ neighborhoods so distributions, mobile pantries, and the right product mix can be aimed where need concentrates.

Consistent everywhere. Every food bank's map is generated by the same pipeline from U.S. Census data — same method, same look, same scoring — so any two are directly comparable.

U.S. Census ACS 5-year (tract) via Census Reporter · place names via the U.S. Census geocoder · Food-need score = min-max composite of poverty, <200% FPL, SNAP, and inverse median income across the region's residential tracts. Illustrative — not a Feeding America product.
