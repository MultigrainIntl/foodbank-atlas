# foodbank-atlas — Claude Code standing rules

> **JoieOS governance applies to this project.**
> Canonical source: `MultigrainIntl/ai-project-operating-system` — `joieos/JOIEOS.md` (authority,
> execution, memory) and `joieos/JOIEOS_PROTOCOL.md` (philosophy). Local copy for Claude Code:
> `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`.
>
> Loop: **Observe → Report → Approve → Patch → Verify → Stop.** One gate at a time.
> Nothing deployed, pushed to a live branch, merged, or deleted without GAJ's explicit approval.
> "Built" is not "working"; missing proof means Incomplete, Blocked or Failed — never "ready".
> Same-actor testing is IMPLEMENTATION_TESTED, never VERIFIED.
>
> Project-specific rules below may narrow this protocol. They may never weaken its proof,
> approval, safety, privacy, or deployment protections.
